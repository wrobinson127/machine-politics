REG-SOFT since 2022-06-14, EXPLICIT

Supports new non-binding measures.

The UK's published position ("Ambitious, Safe, Responsible", 2022) renounces fully autonomous weapon systems and opposes systems operating without meaningful, context-appropriate human involvement, while pursuing responsible-use measures rather than a new binding instrument: the UK endorses the US Political Declaration and the REAIM instruments, votes yes on the UNGA resolutions, and is absent from the September 2025 ready-to-negotiate statement that France and Germany joined. The position has been stable across a change of government; the "no intention" formulation runs back to 2013.

The UK renounces possession and development of fully autonomous weapon systems.

the United Kingdom does not possess fully autonomous weapon systems and has no intention of developing them

Ambitious, Safe, Responsible: our approach to the delivery of AI-enabled capability in Defence, 2022-06-14 source

The UK opposes systems operating without meaningful human involvement across their lifecycle.

the creation and use of systems that would operate without meaningful and context-appropriate human involvement throughout their lifecycle

Ambitious, Safe, Responsible: our approach to the delivery of AI-enabled capability in Defence, 2022-06-14 source

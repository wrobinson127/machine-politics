Denmark

DNK · DENMARK · on the world map

What does it want?

Position: unclear or shifting (AMBIG). Has said things that point in more than one direction.

Unclear or shifting (AMBIG), since 2024-03-04, points both ways (AMBIGUOUS)

In March 2024 Denmark co-wrote, with six other states, a paper saying the future instrument should prohibit four classes of autonomous weapons and regulate the rest. The paper does not say whether that instrument should be binding. In its own words at the expert group, Denmark backed the text as a basis in line with a mandate that does not prejudge the instrument's nature, and said it appreciated that the report's recommendations are not binding treaty texts. It has not said, in any record opened here, that it wants a binding instrument. So the record shows prohibitions on one side and an open form on the other. Denmark voted Yes on all three UN resolutions.

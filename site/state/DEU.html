Supports a legally binding instrument that includes prohibitions.

Germany's national contribution to the Secretary-General's report supports a legally binding instrument in the CCW GGE that prohibits lethal autonomous weapons systems that cannot comply with international humanitarian law, and separately supports an additional CCW protocol prohibiting LAWS operating outside human control, with regulation for other systems. The human-decision principle is stated in its own words.

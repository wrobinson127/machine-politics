Greece

GRC · GREECE · on the world map

What does it want?

Position: unclear or shifting (AMBIG). Has said things that point in more than one direction.

Unclear or shifting (AMBIG), since 2023-10-23, points both ways (AMBIGUOUS)

Greece has said since 2023 that it supports the two-tier approach, which bans some autonomous weapons and regulates the rest, and that the Geneva weapons convention, the CCW, is the right place for it. It has spoken of shaping an international instrument and of further codifying international law, but it has never said whether that instrument should be binding. So its words point toward prohibitions while leaving the form open.

Greece supports the two-tier approach discussed in the Geneva expert group.

Greece supports the two-tier approach expressed by many countries during the deliberations of the GGE on LAWS in Geneva

UNGA First Committee, 78th session, thematic debate on conventional weapons: Statement of Greece, 2023-10-23 source

Greece will help shape an international instrument but does not say what kind.

Greece will actively participate in future deliberations on this complex issue and in the shaping of an international instrument

Lethal autonomous weapons systems: report of the Secretary-General (A/79/88), 2024-05-23 source

Greece calls for further codification of international law under the CCW.

fulfil the mandate of the GGE on LAWS in a timely manner and to work towards the further codification of international law under the CCW

UNGA First Committee, 80th session, Cluster 3 (Conventional Weapons): Statement of Greece, 2025-10-24 source

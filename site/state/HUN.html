Keep it in Geneva (CCW-ONLY), since 2025-10-13, stated directly (EXPLICIT)

At the UN First Committee in October 2025, Hungary said it remains committed to advancing the work of the Geneva expert group on autonomous weapons. It did not say what that work should produce, so on this site's scale it backs the Geneva process without a stated outcome.

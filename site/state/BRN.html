Brunei

BRN · BRUNEI DARUSSALAM · on the world map

What does it want?

Position: unclear or shifting (AMBIG). Has said things that point in more than one direction.

Unclear or shifting (AMBIG), since 2024-10-15, points both ways (AMBIGUOUS)

Brunei's own words point two ways. In the UN First Committee's 2024 general debate it said autonomous weapons should not operate without strict regulation and oversight, and it took note of the Secretary-General's call for a legally binding instrument to prohibit them by 2026. It did not say it wants that treaty, or what kind of rules it prefers.

Brunei points to the Secretary-General's call for a binding instrument to prohibit LAWS by 2026.

Brunei Darussalam takes note of the recommendation made by the Secretary-General in 2023 to develop a legally binding instrument to prohibit LAWS by 2026

National statement by Brunei Darussalam, First Committee general debate, 79th session, 2024-10-15 source

Brunei warns against LAWS operating without strict regulatory frameworks.

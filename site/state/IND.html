Keep it in Geneva (CCW-ONLY), since 2024-12-02, stated directly (EXPLICIT)

Supports continuing the consensus process in the Geneva weapons convention, and has not said what it should produce.

India holds that the Geneva convention's expert group is the right forum and that the UN General Assembly track duplicates it. In its 2024 statement explaining its vote, India said it abstained because parallel processes duplicate effort while the Geneva group already covers the full range of issues. India's votes ran No, Abstain, Yes across the three resolutions. That is a change in how it treats the UN resolutions, not a change in which forum it wants, so no position shift is recorded. Whether the 2025 Yes signals a softer position is an open question; India's 2025 statement explaining that vote, not yet obtained, would answer it.

India abstained on the 2024 resolution, stating its reason in its own explanation of vote.

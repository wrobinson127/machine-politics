CCW-ONLY since 2024-12-02, EXPLICIT

Supports the CCW/GGE consensus process only, no stated outcome position.

India holds that the CCW GGE is the appropriate forum and that the UNGA track duplicates it: its 2024 explanation of vote states it abstained because parallel mandates duplicate efforts and resources, while the GGE addresses the full range of challenges. The vote trajectory (No in 2023, Abstain in 2024, Yes in 2025) is a change in posture toward the UNGA resolutions; the research record shows no change in the underlying forum preference, so no position shift event is coded. Whether the 2025 Yes reflects substantive softening is an open question that the unretrieved 2025 explanation of vote decides.

India abstained on the 2024 resolution, stating its reason in its own explanation of vote.

India has abstained on the resolution L.77

Explanation of vote by India on resolution 79/62 (L.77), Lethal autonomous weapons systems, November 2024 source

India objects to parallel UNGA mandates as duplicating the CCW GGE's work.

Parallel mandates and processes on lethal autonomous weapons systems also amount to a duplication of efforts and resources

In its own 2025 working paper India supports prohibiting the use of fully autonomous LAWS that cannot comply with IHL.

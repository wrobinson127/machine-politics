Machine Politics records where every country stands on autonomous weapons: recorded votes, official statements, and national policy, tracked as they shift over time.

The site takes no position on whether autonomous weapons should be banned or regulated. It records who says what.

## Who makes this

Machine Politics is researched and written by Walker Robinson, the analyst of record for every coding on this site. He works on AI compliance for autonomous weapons systems and writes on autonomous weapons policy. More of his work is at [walker-robinson.com](https://walker-robinson.com). <!-- FLAG: URL inferred from this site's own domain (machinepolitics.walker-robinson.com). Every coded position on this site traces to a quoted, dated, linked primary source, and every one was approved by the analyst of record before it rendered. The [methodology page](methodology.html) explains the rubric, the evidence rules, and what the site does when the record is ambiguous.

## What this costs

$0 infrastructure. Static pages, no tracking, no ads, no accounts. The coding is human analyst work. The pipeline makes no model calls.

## Contact and corrections

If you find an error, start at the [corrections page](corrections.html); it explains the policy. Report it by opening an issue in the repository. Corrections are handled in public, on the record, rather than by private mail.
